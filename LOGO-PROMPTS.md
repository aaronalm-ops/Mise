# Mise logo prompts (27 Oct 2026)

Shortlist chosen by Aaron: 1 Stoneware Stamp, 3 Fork m, 5 Bowl and Steam, 6 Ladle Line. Paste each block as is into ChatGPT or Gemini.

## Option 1: The Stoneware Stamp
```
Design a premium app logo for a meal planning app called "mise" (from the French kitchen phrase "mise en place").

Concept: a maker's seal pressed into the base of a handmade ceramic pot. A circular stamp with the word "mise" in lowercase, debossed (pressed in) into matte stoneware clay.

Style and finish:
- Matte stoneware texture with a fine, even grain, like unglazed ceramic.
- Colours: graphite clay #3A3D41 for the surface, the pressed-in letters and seal ring in off-white #F4F6F8.
- A soft, subtle inner shadow where the stamp is pressed into the clay. Slightly imperfect, hand-pressed edges.
- No gloss, no shine, no gradients, no reflections, no 3D bevels, no drop shadows outside the shape.
- Lettering: a clean, rounded, modern lowercase sans-serif, generous letter spacing. Spell it exactly "mise". No serif fonts.
- Calm, premium, crafted, minimal. Plenty of empty space around the seal.

Output: a square 1:1 image, 1024 x 1024 pixels, the seal centred and filling about 60 percent of the frame, on a plain graphite #3A3D41 background. Show 4 different variations of this concept.
```

## Option 3: The Fork m
```
Design a premium app logo for a meal planning app called "mise".

Concept: a single lowercase letter "m" whose three downward strokes end as the tines of a fork. The letter and the fork are one shape, so it reads as both "m" and a fork at a glance.

Style and finish:
- A heavy, rounded, custom lowercase "m" with even stroke weight and soft rounded corners. The tines are cut cleanly and simply at the bottom of the three strokes.
- Colours: off-white #F4F6F8 symbol on a graphite #3A3D41 background.
- Matte finish with a very fine, subtle ceramic grain across the background and the symbol.
- Flat design: no gloss, no gradients, no shine, no 3D, no outlines, no drop shadows.
- Geometric, confident, modern, premium. It must stay clear and recognisable at very small sizes, like a phone app icon.
- No other text, no extra objects, no plates or food.

Output: a square 1:1 image, 1024 x 1024 pixels, the symbol centred and filling about 55 percent of the frame. Show 4 different variations of this concept.
```

## Option 6: The Ladle Line

```
- Matte finish with a very fine, subtle ceramic grain across the background.

```
