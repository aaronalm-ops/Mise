Same family as Lumen.

Manrope titles, Inter body, JetBrains Mono for quantities and labels so 750 g never jumps. White used generously, charcoal for primary controls, one accent. Hairlines instead of shadows.

Matte Stone.

Graphite (#3A3D41) on warm off-white by day, silver (#C7CBD1) on black by night, with a fine grain: no gloss, no gradients, no colour accent. Colour comes only from food photos. Cyan stays only in the maker mark.

Instinct navigation.

Approved lifted disc: 48 px, 12 px rise, 300 ms ease-out, flat matte graphite fill. Four destinations plus a filled Add action in the centre (new recipe, manual list item, photo to recipe later).

Shared list cues.

Each ticked item shows who ticked it (J or A). "Live" confirms both phones are in sync; offline it would read "Offline · will sync".
